About Skincare

Skincare is a personal journey that involves maintaining skin integrity, enhancing its appearance, and relieving skin conditions through the careful application of cosmetic products.

Key Concepts

Skin Barrier: The outermost layer of your skin that protects against environmental damage.

Hydration vs. Moisture: Hydration adds water to the skin, while moisture locks it in.

Patch Testing: A crucial method for checking how your skin reacts to new products.

Physical scrubs are largely being replaced by chemical exfoliants in modern routines.

Example Rule: Thinnest to thickest consistency

Shortcut: SPF 30+ is your best defense against aging.

Term: TEWL occurs when your skin barrier is compromised.

  2. Apply Vitamin C Serum (optional)
  3. Moisturize
  4. Apply Sunscreen (SPF 30+)

Star Ingredients and Their Uses

Hyaluronic Acid
A humectant that draws water into the skin, plumping fine lines.
Niacinamide (Vitamin B3)
Soothes redness, regulates oil production, and brightens tone.
Salicylic Acid (BHA)
An oil-soluble acid that clears out pores and fights acne.

Rules for Combining Active Ingredients
  • Don't mix: Retinol and AHA/BHA (at the same time)
  • Don't mix: Vitamin C and Benzoyl Peroxide
  • Do mix: Retinol and Ceramides/Hyaluronic Acid
  • Do mix: Vitamin C and Sunscreen
How to Patch Test
  1. Apply a small amount of product to a clear patch of skin (like the inner arm).
  2. Wait 24 hours.
  3. Check for redness, itching, or swelling.
  4. If no reaction occurs, apply to the face carefully.

⚠️ Sun Sensitivity Warning

Using chemical exfoliants (AHAs/BHAs) or retinoids increases your skin's sensitivity to the sun.

You must apply a broad-spectrum sunscreen (SPF 30 or higher) every morning while using these products to prevent severe damage and hyperpigmentation.
